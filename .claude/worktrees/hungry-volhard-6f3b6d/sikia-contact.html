Before you write — common ones

Is Sikia Law free for students? +
Yes. The full library — case law, statutes, SIs, decrees, legal notices, lecture notes — is free for any Ugandan law student. No card, no trial, no ad-reads. Just a university email.
A case I need isn't in the library — what now? +
Email library@sikialaw.ug with the citation. Our librarians add missing cases within one working day, usually within hours. You'll get a reply when it's up.
Can I use Sikia Law if I'm not a Ugandan student? +
Yes. The library is open to practitioners, researchers, journalists, and the public. Students from other East African universities are welcome too — the practitioner plan is the closest fit.
How do I report a broken link or a wrong citation? +
Use the form above and pick "Broken link or bug". Paste the URL if you can. We take breakage seriously — our credibility is the library.
Can my law school partner with Sikia Law? +
Very much yes. We offer institutional partnerships that cover every student on your roll, with a lecturer portal for reading lists. Write to press@sikialaw.ug.
